OFFICIAL COMPETITION RULE

Drone Soccer Class 20 — 3×3 (Brushed)

20 cm Drone Soccer, 3-player format (Brushed motors)

Open Original PDF ↗
Players3 players
AgeOpen / No age limit
Drone20±1 cm
Weight≤ 110 g
MotorBrushed only
ArenaApprox. 3×6 m
01 · Competitor Eligibility
  1. Each team has 3 members, with all 3 competing in each match.
  2. There is no age limit for competitors.
  3. A team may have 1 team manager (or none). The team manager may not participate in the match.
  4. A competitor may not be registered on another team in the same competition category.
02 · Drone Requirements
  1. Any brand of spherical quadcopter may be used. Drone diameter must be 20 cm ±1 cm and weight must not exceed 110 g, including the battery but excluding the remote controller.
  2. Brushed motors only.
  3. The drone must be remotely controlled with a radio controller.
  4. The battery must have a clearly readable information label. Batteries with missing or illegible labels are not permitted.
  5. The drone must be presented to the officials for inspection before every match.
03 · Competition Rules
  1. Two teams compete at a time: Red and Blue. Each drone must show a clearly visible team LED color or marking, and player positions must be declared to the officials so the Striker can be tagged.
  2. A match consists of 3 sets of 3 minutes each, best 2 of 3. Set duration may be reduced to 2 minutes if there are many participating teams.
  3. Players may take off only after the official gives the start signal.
  4. Only the Striker may score, by flying through the opponent’s goal ring.
  5. After the Striker scores, the goal LED lights up. All players must retreat behind the Center Line before the score is confirmed. A new scoring attempt may begin once the LED turns off; a goal made before the LED turns off will not count.
  6. If any player can no longer continue flying, the player must put down the controller and raise both hands to notify the officials.
  7. At the end of the set, the official blows the whistle and competitors must land and stop the propellers.
  8. Players may not enter the arena until time has expired and an official allows them to retrieve the drones.
  9. The team with more valid goals is the winner.

Additional Provisions

  1. If a team intentionally interferes with an opponent’s flight and causes a flight accident, that team will be disqualified.
  2. All rulings, decisions, and judgments of the officials are final.
04 · Competition Arena
  1. Indoor competition in an arena approximately 3×6 m, enclosed by safety netting.
  2. The goal is a ring approximately 30–37 cm in diameter.
  3. Scoring: the drone must fly completely through the goal to score.
  4. Player positions in the rulebook: STRIKER (scorer), SWEEPER (midfield), and KEEPER (goalkeeper). The SWEEPER and KEEPER may not score.
05 · Responsibility & Notes

Responsibility

  1. Each participating team is responsible for the safety of its own drone and for accidents caused by its members or drones.
  2. The organizer is not responsible for incidents and/or accidents arising from participation or competitors’ equipment.

Notes

  • If the competition rules change, the committee will announce the changes on the competition day.
  • Officials will explain the rules again before competition begins.

OFFICIAL COMPETITION RULE

Super Pilot — Junior

Super Pilot category, age 12 or under

Open Original PDF ↗
Competitor1 competitor
Age≤ 12 years
Birth yearB.E. 2557 onward
DroneSkykick 2 / EVO
Time3 minutes
ArenaApprox. 4×8 m
01 · Competitor Eligibility
  1. Each entry consists of 1 competitor.
  2. Competitors must be age 12 or under. Age is calculated by birth year relative to competition year B.E. 2569, without using birth month or day — born in B.E. 2557 or later.
  3. One team manager is allowed (or none).
02 · Drone Requirements
  1. Only Skykick 2 / Skykick EVO drones may be used. Drone diameter is 20 cm and weight must not exceed 120 g, including the battery but excluding the remote controller.
  2. The drone must be remotely controlled with a radio controller.
  3. Only the battery model specified for the Skykick drone may be used; modifications are not permitted.
  4. The battery must have a clearly readable information label. Batteries with missing or illegible labels are not permitted.
  5. The drone must be presented to the officials for inspection before every match.
03 · Competition Rules
  1. Before the run, the competitor must submit the drone for inspection by the officials.
  2. After the start signal, the competitor has 3 minutes to fly through the obstacles following the flight guideline, circle the poles, and complete 10 ring goals before the timer stops.
  3. The drone may touch obstacles during the run, but must not damage them.
  4. At 3 minutes, the official blows the whistle and the competitor must land.
  5. Each competitor makes 2 attempts. The better result — completing the full course in the shortest time — is used for ranking. If results are exactly tied, a rerun is required.
  6. Players may not enter the arena until time has expired and an official allows them to retrieve the drones.
  7. Any competitor who attempts to interfere with another competitor’s flight and causes a flight accident will be disqualified.
  8. All rulings, decisions, and judgments of the officials are final.
04 · Competition Arena
  1. Indoor competition in an arena approximately 4×8 m, enclosed by safety netting.
  2. The ring goal is approximately 30–37 cm in diameter.
  3. Obstacle poles are approximately 2 m high.
  4. The flight guideline and top-view arena layout are shown in the original rulebook.
05 · Responsibility & Notes

Responsibility

  1. Each participating team is responsible for the safety of its own drone and for accidents caused by its members or drones.
  2. The organizer is not responsible for incidents and/or accidents arising from participation or competitors’ equipment.

Notes

  • If the competition rules change, the committee will announce the changes on the competition day.
